## 👨‍💻 About me

I work across the full delivery path of enterprise systems — from domain modelling and API design through to deployment, observability and long-term maintenance.

My focus areas are academic and institutional platform modernisation, high-integrity transactional systems, and the architecture work that lets a small team ship reliably: clean service boundaries, a normalised and well-indexed data model, cache strategy that matches real read patterns, and automated verification at every layer.

I care most about the parts of a system that are hard to retrofit — correctness of the data model, clarity of the API contract, and operational visibility. Those decisions determine whether a platform can absorb five years of changing requirements or has to be rewritten.

## 🎯 Principles

- **Clarity** — A design is finished when the next engineer can predict how it behaves without reading every line.
- **Ownership** — The person who ships a change owns its behaviour in production, not just its merge.
- **Resilience** — Assume dependencies fail. Design timeouts, fallbacks and recovery before the incident, not during it.
- **Quality** — Constraints in the database, validation at the boundary, and tests that would actually fail if the logic broke.
- **Accountability** — Decisions are written down with their trade-offs so they can be revisited on evidence rather than memory.
